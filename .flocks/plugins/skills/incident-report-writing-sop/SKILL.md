# Incident Report Writing SOP

Reports must be understandable by both security engineers and customer management.

## Structure

- Event overview.
- Impacted assets.
- Related vulnerabilities and risk.
- Alerts and evidence.
- Attack analysis.
- Risk level and confidence.
- Recommended actions.
- Follow-up items.

## Writing Rules

- Separate observed evidence from analysis and assumptions.
- Avoid overstating compromise when only probes or alerts exist.
- State uncertainty directly when evidence is incomplete.
- Use concise business impact language.
- Keep recommendations specific, safe and operational.

Never include exploit instructions or destructive response steps.
